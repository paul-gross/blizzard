Board cards and list rows take their project's color as their background, and the list's step and runner columns merge
into one. Runners read `host/runner` wherever their host is known. The chunk detail header names the chunk's project and
gathers every chunk action under its ⋯ menu, Pause and Resume among them. A chunk's graph is no longer changed from the
detail's Graph fact.
