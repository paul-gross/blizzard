The hub board and the runner panel now draw chunk cards and list rows from one shared design, coloured by the chunk's
status. A hub card that is ready or of unknown status reads dim and a waiting one amber, and the runner panel's rows
take their status colour. Board column counts and beacons, the runner panel's lane counts, the glance strip's vitals and
timeline verdicts share the same colours: the waiting count reads bright amber and the glance strip's running vital
amber.
