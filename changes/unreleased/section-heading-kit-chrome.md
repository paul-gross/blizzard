Some section headings on the board now use the shared section-heading look: a project's linked groups, the routine
panel's Recent runs, Related scopes, Inflow & outflow, Measurement and Last swept, the Delivery drawer's Repositories
and Delivery history, and the Fleet page's "No host" divider can differ in size, weight, tracking or spacing. The
spacing under a heading also shifts slightly on those, on a scope's Tended by, on a runner host's Next, and on Admin's
Revisions and related-record headings, the edit dialog's Will change and the change history's What changed.
