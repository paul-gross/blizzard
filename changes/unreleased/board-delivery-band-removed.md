The board no longer shows the Delivery band above its lanes; the Delivery tab carries each chunk's delivery state
instead. A chunk's timeline no longer lists its flow submissions, turns and landings, and a flow's record no longer
opens in the dock; `?flow=` links open the board without it.

## Upgrade

Read a branch's line and a chunk's delivery on the Delivery tab; a saved `?flow=` link opens the board alone.
