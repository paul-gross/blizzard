A runner on OpenCode 2 stops any turn a crash left running on the operator's service: each start, before any restart
resume, interrupts every session its own records hold unfinished with no live worker and no takeover, and
`blizzard runner host` prints how many turns it stopped. A Claude Code or OpenCode 1 worker takes its turn with it when
it dies, so their sessions are left as they are and never counted.
