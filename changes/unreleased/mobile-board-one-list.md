The phone's board is one list of compact cards, read the way the desktop list reads its rows: chunks that need a person,
then those in flight, READY over a divider over BACKLOG, then the day's finished work. Each card carries its lane icon,
its project tint, its node and runner, its cost, any open gate and any open ask. The vitals strip adds READY and DONE
TODAY and drops the live indicator.
