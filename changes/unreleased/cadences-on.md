Turns cadences on. A cadence keeps routines on a rhythm, and the hub now mints each due errand's run on its schedule and
spacing. A hub that wants the old behaviour sets `cadences = false` under `[flags]` in `blizzard-hub.toml`. The operator
page on cadences gains worked cadences for the mutation-testing corpus pass, the ideation routine, and a gardening
trend.
