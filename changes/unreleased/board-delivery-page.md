The board gains a Delivery tab between Board and Fleet: one list of every chunk delivering or recently delivered, with
how far each has reached (PR open, in line and its place, landing, landed), the cause and text of its latest bounce, and
its PRs and landed commits. Selecting a row opens the chunk's delivery history — each queueing, landing attempt, bounce
with its log, and move back through its graph.
