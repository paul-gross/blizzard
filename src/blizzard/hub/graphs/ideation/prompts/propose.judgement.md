# Propose — judgement

Choose `proposed` if the `docket` asset holds at least one proposal, each carrying `findings: []`.

Choose `none` if nothing on the shortlist warrants a proposal yet — everything is minor, speculative past the point of
being worth a person's attention, or already covered by a proposal drafted earlier this same run. The republished
`delta` still delivers; its measurement is the run's product even when the docket is empty.

Prefer `none` over padding the docket. A run that proposes nothing and records what it saw is more useful than one
that manufactures an idea so the pass looks productive.

Either way, `delta` must already be republished with its measurement corrected before you choose: delivery reads that
artifact regardless of which choice you make.
